About Pi Network | Our Mission

Pi Network exists to bring everyday Batswana into the digital economy not as spectators, but as owners. We believe access to a global financial system shouldn't depend on expensive hardware, a foreign bank account, or a computer science degree. It should be as simple as opening an app.



Our Team

Network is built by a team of Botswana-based engineers, designers, and financial-inclusion advocates, working alongside advisors experienced in blockchain infrastructure and regulatory compliance.

Why Botswana, Why Now

Botswana has one of the clearer regulatory paths for virtual assets on the continent, with the Virtual Assets Act giving the industry a defined legal framework rather than a grey area. That stability matters, it means Pi Network can be built openly, in step with local law, instead of around it. At the same time, mobile penetration across Botswana keeps climbing while traditional banking access lags behind in many villages and smaller towns. A mobile-first digital currency fits the country as it actually is today.
