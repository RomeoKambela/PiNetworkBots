Pi Network

Pi Network is a community-powered cryptocurrency that allows users to mine Thebe, a digital currency, using their mobile devices. Our mission is to create a more inclusive and accessible financial system for everyone.

Explore Services

Decentralized

Secure, transparent, community-owned digital money — built on the same principle of open consensus as the traditional kgotla gathering, where every voice has a place at the table.

Community-Owned

Unlike traditional financial systems, Pi Network is owned and governed by its community of users, ensuring that the platform remains aligned with the needs and values of those who use it.

Accessible

Pi Network aims to provide a financial system that is accessible to everyone, regardless of their location or economic background. Our mobile app makes it easy for anyone to participate in the Pi Network ecosystem.
